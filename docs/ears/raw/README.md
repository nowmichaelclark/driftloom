# Raw ears measurements

`pile-2-first-half-raw.tar.gz` (3.5 MB): every album.py output from Ears 6 except audio and stems. Six albums (I Care Because You Do, Ventolin, Donkey Rhubarb, Hangable Auto Bulb, GAK, Analogue Bubblebath 4), 128 files, one folder per album:

- `pick.json`: the picker's mix-level row for every track (all tracks, not just the deep pass) and the picks
- `<track>/events.json`, `techniques.json`, `theory.json`, `space.json`: the full per-track measurements the cards summarize (note groups with every fingerprint number, all sweeps, all chord and bass readings)
- `stems/<track>/split.json`: which separator ran and how long it took (the stems themselves are not kept; MDX-Net stems, about 6 GB, rebuild with `tools/ears/split.py`)
- `progress.log`, `card.md`: stage timings and the unedited draft card

Unpack with `tar xzf pile-2-first-half-raw.tar.gz -C SOMEWHERE`. Later piles can add their own archive here. The cards in `docs/ears/cards-pile-*.md` stay the readable record.

`pile-2-second-half-raw.tar.gz`: the same for Ears 7 (Demucs htdemucs stems, not MDX-Net). Five albums (Bradley's Beat, Bradley's Robot, Analogue Bubblebath 3, Caustic Window Compilation, Analogue Bubblebath 5), one folder per album, same layout as above (`pick.json`, `<track>/{events,techniques,theory,space,stem_check}.json`, `stems/<track>/split.json`, `progress.log`, `card.md`). `stem_check.json` is new since Ears 6b: each track's stem shares, band shares, strongest low lines, drum-onset grid and the summed-stems floor-drop runs. `split.json` here says `demucs-htdemucs` and the seconds it took. No audio, stems or joined wavs. The cards are in `docs/ears/cards-pile-2.md` (second half). Its `ears7-session/` folder (added 2026-10-18, when `~/ears7` was deleted) holds the album.py chain script and log, the working card drafts and the 20 s separator test. Unpack with `tar xzf pile-2-second-half-raw.tar.gz -C SOMEWHERE`.

`ears8-synthesis-raw.tar.gz` (29 KB): the first synthesis's measurements (Ears 8, 2026-10-18), behind `docs/ears/batch-5.md`. No audio: the render2.mjs index of every final render (sketch draws, floor drops, refused and asked notes per voice), `sketch_check.py`, `low_guard.py` and `stereo_guard.py` outputs, `kick_check.py` on Ears 7's record stems, the clip plan and timestamps, the corpus rates of each sketch, and every job list. Since 2026-10-18 (58 KB) it also holds `work/`: the session's helper scripts, render logs, selection and null checks and the clip timestamps, kept when `~/ears8` was deleted. Its `README.txt` lists the files.

`pile-3-raw.tar.gz` (3.9 MB): the same for Ears 9 (2026-10-25, Demucs htdemucs stems). Seven records (Richard D. James Album, Girl/Boy EP, Come to Daddy, 51/13 Aphex Singles Collection, Windowlicker EP, 2 Remixes by AFX, Drukqs), 201 files, one folder per album: `pick.json` (every track's mix-level row and the picks, edited by hand on three albums: Girl_Boy Song added to Richard D. James Album, the NLS mix dropped from Girl/Boy, 51/13 limited to On, Crowsmangegus, Donkey Rhubarb and 73-Yips), `<track>/{events,techniques,theory,space,stem_check}.json`, `stems/<track>/split.json`, `progress.log` and the unedited draft `card.md`. No audio, stems or joined wavs. Unpack with `tar xzf pile-3-raw.tar.gz -C SOMEWHERE`. The pile 1 records have no archive here (their numbers are on `cards-pile-1.md` only), which is why `twins.py` could not catch 51/13's *On* and *73-Yips* as repeats of pile 1.

`pile-4-raw.tar.gz` (1.7 MB): the same for Ears 10 (2026-11-02, Demucs htdemucs; tracks over 11 minutes split in 10-minute pieces). Four records carded (Structures from Silence, Kankyo Ongaku, View From Above, Enchanted Instrumentals and Whispers), one folder per album, 124 files: `pick.json`, `<track>/{events,techniques,theory,space,stem_check}.json`, `stems/<track>/split.json`, `progress.log`, the unedited draft `card.md`, and the new `longform/<track>.json` (`tools/ears/longform.py`: per-window series, swells, sections, returns, a line a minute). View From Above also has `longform_whole/whole_album.json` (the 73:40 hour as one piece). On Structures from Silence, 01's `stem_check.json` and 03's `stem_check.json` and `theory.json` cover a 10-minute window (the field `window_s`). The 2814 and Mirror's Edge folders hold only `pick.json` and `progress.log`: Ears 10b's picks, whose `paths` point at joined wavs that are not kept, so 10b planned to rerun the pick (in the end it reused them from the local cache). `ears10-session/` holds the chain scripts, their logs, the summary helpers and the chunked-Demucs test. Unpack with `tar xzf pile-4-raw.tar.gz -C SOMEWHERE`.

`pile-4b-raw.tar.gz` (0.9 MB): the same for Ears 10b (2026-11-03, Demucs htdemucs): 2814 *Birth of a New Day* and *Mirror's Edge*, 53 work files in the layout above (the title track split in two 10-minute pieces), plus `moments/<track>.json` for Mirror's Edge (each stem entry, exit and drop: its length and where it lands on the kick grid). `ears10b-session/` holds the chain script and log, the four helper scripts (`moments.py`, `beatfit.py`, `monoloss.py`, `lrbal.py`) and their outputs, the `kick_check.py` lines and the summary tables. No audio, stems or joined wavs. Unpack with `tar xzf pile-4b-raw.tar.gz -C SOMEWHERE`.
