New in v19: next picks

The chosen design (design/next-picks.md), built into the app (build v97). Pick Now Playing, no album, New loops or Length above, or press leave album on Now Playing.

  • newNow Playing outside an album drops the "Next: …" line and shows what new loops lean toward as outline pills: moods on the first row, tempo and length on the second. Only picked values show; with nothing picked, a dashed "anything". The pills are one button that opens New loops. In an album, Now Playing is as before.
  • newNew loops: five lines (moods; tempo and length; key; style; voices), a close key, and an any key per line, lit while the whole line is left to chance. Moods open the Vibes list; length opens the Length picker. Tempo, key, style and voices have no picker yet.
  • newLength: cancel / Length / done; −1, the number (tap to type), +1; a doubling slider with sixteen stops from 1 to 32,768; an Endless switch. Cancel puts back the length from before, "any" included.
  • newEach line has a colour that outlines its pills (and colours the length slider and switch); the light themes take a darker set. Text stays in the theme's text colours.
  • noteBaked picks: merry, tender, 70–85 bpm, 16 bars, as in the brief. The any keys, −1, +1, the slider and Endless change picks, so here they show a note; try them in the app.

What changed from v17 to v18

v17 was drawn by hand and then brought into the app. The app has moved on since, and v18 shows it as it is now. Against v17:

  • changedOne phone instead of nine. The theme is picked inside it, in Library › More, as in the app.
  • changedBottom keys. No LEDs and no latched look; only Play and the Sound / Layers switch change their icons. Row 3 is two keys: Now Playing, and the switch (Mikey's choice when the design came into the app).
  • changedNo drawn status-bar gap. The Info zone's 40px top padding made room for a drawn status bar; in the app the inset does that, so the card sits 10px below it. The Info zone is shorter and the Dynamic zone taller (see the table).
  • changedThe Info card. Real cover art. Its lines are the app's readout: up to three styles and feelings, key, tempo, meter and bars, a form word (airy, drifting) and the album that is playing. When they do not fit, the lesser feelings and styles fold away (ui.js), where v17's lines took turns.
  • newNow Playing has the Vibes key at its top left; Vibes is a view of its own.
  • newSound (seven sliders), Save (tick the albums this loop is in) and Share are drawn, where v17 left them unbuilt.
  • newThe Layers rows follow the playhead bar by bar, as in the app.
  • fixedIn the app: the key press, and reduced motion. Two reduced-motion blocks in the app's stylesheet had been damaged when v17 was brought in. The first had lost its opening line, so its "keys do not sink" rule applied to everybody (no key sank when pressed) while its "nothing animates" rule did nothing (the light themes' glint and every transition kept running under reduced motion). The second had lost its declarations, so the keys inside the panel still dropped under reduced motion. Both are put back as v17 had them (build v96).

Measured from the app

Rules that carry over from v17

These held for the v17 drawings and the app was built to them. They have not been re-measured on the app yet (the v17 test kit measures the v17 file); that is the next step. One has already been found broken and fixed in this session: the reduced-motion rules (below).

  • Three zones, fixed heights; nothing pops up. Only lists scroll: the Library's lists, Advanced, the Vibes list and Save's albums.
  • Two settings: Theme and Lighter on processor. One behavior for everybody.
  • Text is 11px or larger and in rem (the status bar excepted). Numbers stay with their words (no-break spaces).
  • Every control is a native button with a name. On/off is a switch; Play swaps its name between Play and Pause.
  • Contrast is measured from pixels: text 4.5:1; notes, beat cells, the lit cell and focus rings 3:1. Off-beat cells and empty-slot dots are decorative.
  • Light-theme notes keep their chroma; Olive keeps to warm hues.
  • Reduced motion: nothing slides, fades or twinkles, and pressed keys keep their darker fill but do not drop. The playhead still steps with the music. Every mark drawn as a background color has a forced-colors rule.
  • Touch targets: roll and on/off are drawn at 48 × 48, with no invisible hit pads.

Open items

  • decideNext picks: the way in during an album. The pills are hidden in an album, so they can't open New loops there. Suggestion from the brief: keep the top-left key, renamed "new loops", in an album only. Until then the Vibes key stays as it was.
  • decideNext picks: setting only tempo or only length back to "any". Line two's any key clears both.
  • nextMeasure the app against the standard. Port the v17 test kit to this file (one phone, a theme at a time). It is the same as measuring the app, since this file is the app's own drawing.
  • decideThe Info card drops lines when it is full. v17's rule was that nothing in it is ever dropped; lines took turns instead (the WCAG 2.2.2 mechanism). The app folds the lesser feelings and styles away. Keep the app's way, or bring the turns back?
  • foundThe Info card can cut off its last lines. Its text column is as tall as the art (118px). With the sample loop the readout fits at 100%, but from 130% the meter line and the album line are hidden, even after folding as far as it goes (one style and one feeling leave nothing to fold; the album and meter lines never fold). The audit line says so when it happens. It is the app's own behavior, not a baking artifact.
  • openThe Play / Pause glyph is under 3:1 in Dawn, Tidal, Olive and Night Light (found in v17, not yet fixed).
  • laterSafari, Firefox, VoiceOver and TalkBack, once the design is locked.

How this file is made

It is baked: design/test-kit/bake.mjs runs the real app in headless Chromium at 340 × 780, seeds a library (30 loops, five albums, from fixed seeds), plays one of the albums, visits every view at each text size, and copies what the app drew. The phone's stylesheet is css/style.css itself. So to change the look, change the app and bake again; to change this page, edit design/test-kit/spec.template.html.
