Advanced

            

              

                
Sound

                
Mono
                  
Plays one channel instead of stereo, for a single speaker or one earbud. The music is the same, only narrower.

                  

              

              

                
Processor

                
Lite
                  
Lighter on the processor. Uses less of the phone, so older ones keep up. A busy loop may play a few notes fewer. Turns itself on if this device can't keep up, unless you choose.

                  

                  

              

              

                
Files

                

                  
                  
                  
                  
                

                
Your loops and albums live on this device. A backup is one file, or one text, with all of them.

                
Adds a backup's loops and albums to your Library. Nothing already there is replaced.
